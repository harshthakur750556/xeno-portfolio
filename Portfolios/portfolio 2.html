Behind The Alias

manifesto

XENO is a handle — a name chosen the way an artist signs a canvas. Behind it lives a developer who believes interfaces should feel like places, not pages: seeded with atmosphere, responsive to touch, and quietly alive.

I design and build kinetic web experiences — from shader-lit product sites to generative art systems — obsessing over the last 5% where a machine stops feeling like a machine. Rusty prototypes become artifacts; constraints become the aesthetic.

Offline, I'm usually prototyping in the void: shaders, audio-reactive visuals, and whatever strange geometry the night produces.
