04 · About

The designer behind the alias

sol · noun — the sun

SOL started as a signature on a sketchbook — a short, warm name that would survive being stamped on a business card. Today it's the working identity of Harsh Thakur, a digital designer & creative developer from Prayagraj, India.

I believe good design is invisible orchestration: rhythm in type, restraint in colour, and motion that answers a question before it's asked. My favourite projects are the ones where the interface feels like it was grown, not assembled.

"Make it feel like a place, not a page — then leave room for sunlight."

When I'm not designing, I'm sketching generative art, reading type specimens for fun, and teaching local students that the web can be beautiful.
